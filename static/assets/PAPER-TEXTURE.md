# Paper Texture

`paper-texture.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for this
project. It contains no third-party reference image. It is used as an optional,
locally served light-theme background and is included with the project artwork.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: subtle square seamless tileable
> raster paper texture for the background of a readable household calendar
> dashboard. Primary request: flat, evenly lit clean white uncoated paper seen
> extremely close straight on, subtle naturally random fine paper fibres and
> faint grain only. Neutral white to very pale neutral grey, not beige, not yellow.
> Uniform brightness across the whole surface with no vignette or lighting
> gradient. Edge-to-edge texture; seamless repeat edges. No text, lines, ruled
> marks, objects, corners, torn edges, folds, shadows, stains, logos or watermark.
> Very low contrast; the final texture must stay unobtrusive behind dark calendar
> text.
